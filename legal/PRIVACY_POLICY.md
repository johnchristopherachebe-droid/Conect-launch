# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[YOUR COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("cönëct," "we," "us," or "our")
collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the cönëct app and website
(together, the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information.** Name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a secure
hash, never in plain text).

**Verification data.** We verify your email (via a confirmation link) and phone number (via an
SMS code) before your account can place or fulfill orders. We store whether each is verified, not
the codes themselves once used.

**Location data.** When you set a delivery address or a vendor sets a business location, we
collect the precise coordinates you pin on the map, plus device GPS location if you grant
permission, to calculate delivery distance and fees and to route orders accurately.

**Order and payment information.** Order contents, delivery address, order status, and payment
status. Payments are processed by **Paystack**; we do not receive or store your full card
number, expiry date, or CVV. For vendor/rider payouts, we store the bank name, account number,
and account name you provide, used solely to send payments via Paystack's transfer system.

**Identity and business verification documents.** Riders and vendors are asked to submit a
government-issued ID and, for vendors, business documents (e.g., registration certificate,
business logo) as part of approval. These are reviewed by our admin team and stored to satisfy
verification and fraud-prevention requirements.

**Communications.** Messages sent through in-app chat between customers, vendors, riders, and
support, and any support tickets you open.

**Usage and device data.** Log data such as IP address, device/browser type, and app interaction
data, collected automatically to operate and secure the Service.

**Push notification data.** If you enable notifications, we store a device push subscription
token used only to deliver notifications to your device.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the information above to:
- Create and secure your account, and verify your identity, email, and phone number.
- Process and fulfill orders, calculate delivery fees, and route deliveries.
- Process payments and payouts through Paystack.
- Review and approve vendor and rider applications.
- Provide customer support and respond to disputes or refund requests.
- Send order updates, verification codes, receipts, and (if enabled) push notifications.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service.
- Improve and maintain the Service.

## 3. How We Share Information
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:
- **With vendors and riders**, as needed to fulfill an order (e.g., a rider sees the delivery
  address and customer phone number for an assigned order; a vendor sees the customer's order
  and delivery address).
- **With service providers** who process data on our behalf: Paystack (payments and payouts),
  our SMTP provider (verification and transactional emails), Termii (SMS verification codes),
  and our hosting/database provider.
- **With OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service**, to convert a map location into a readable address
  or to search for a location by name — only the coordinates or search text you enter, no
  account information, are sent for this purpose.
- **For legal reasons**, if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or
  property of cönëct, our users, or the public.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to
  standard confidentiality protections.

## 4. Data Retention
We retain account and order information for as long as your account is active and as needed to
comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Verification documents are retained for the
duration of your approval as a vendor/rider and for a reasonable period afterward for fraud and
compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your account as described in Section 6.

## 5. Data Security
We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including password hashing,
encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access controls limiting who can view sensitive data. No
method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices
Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal
information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can:
- Update your profile information directly in the app.
- Request a copy of your data or account deletion by contacting **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.
- Withdraw push notification permission at any time in your device settings.
- Disable location permissions, though this will limit your ability to place or fulfill orders,
  since a precise delivery/business location is required to use core features of the Service.

If you are located in Nigeria, you may also have rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act
2023. If you are located elsewhere, you may have additional rights under your local law (such as
the GDPR in the European Economic Area/UK) — contact us and we will respond as required by the
law that applies to you.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The Service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information,
contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Service, you consent to your information being transferred to and processed in such countries,
