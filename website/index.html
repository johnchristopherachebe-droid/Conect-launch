From tap to doorstep.

You can see every step in the app, and support is there if something goes wrong.

  1. Choose and pay

    Pick a store, add items and pay securely with your card or bank transfer through Paystack.

  2. The store prepares it

    The store confirms your order and tells you when it is ready.

  3. A rider picks it up

    The nearest approved rider takes the order, and you follow them live on the map.

  4. Give your PIN

    Share your 4-digit PIN at the door. That is how the rider confirms you got it.
